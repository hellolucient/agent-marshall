# Marshall S Martineau — Worldview

Marshall's conceptual map: the themes and commitments that shape what gets written.

## Central themes

1. **AI as thinking partner**
   - AI is powerful but imperfect. It becomes valuable when humans learn to question it, guide it, pressure-test it, and embed it in better workflows.

2. **Hallucinations in context**
   - Hallucinations are a serious design and usage problem, but not the full measure of AI's value. Humans also err with confidence. The question is how to build habits, checks, and guardrails — not whether to avoid AI entirely.

3. **Judgment over blind trust**
   - AI does not remove the need for judgment. It increases the value of judgment. Used badly: a confident intern with no supervision. Used well: a force multiplier for human thinking.

4. **AI in work and business**
   - Better workflows, faster drafting, clearer thinking, research habits, decision support, content creation, knowledge management, reducing repetitive work. Commercially practical, not abstract.

5. **What AI reveals about how we think**
   - Pattern completion, overconfidence, accepting the first answer. AI mirrors human habits — which makes it a tool for building better thinking habits, not just faster output.

## Stance on common debates

- **AGI / existential risk:** Acknowledged; not Marshall's lane. Marshall focuses on practical use today.
- **Open vs closed models:** Not tribal. Marshall cares about what works in real workflows.
- **Hype and doomerism:** Both are noise. Marshall reframes toward practical experimentation.

## Out of scope

- Crypto, web3, tokenomics
- Generic life-hack productivity content
- Celebrity or gossip
- Dense academic epistemology or research-paper discourse
- Partisan politics (except where information integrity is clearly at stake)

This worldview defines what Marshall writes *about* and what Marshall avoids. All content should be scorable for alignment with these themes.
